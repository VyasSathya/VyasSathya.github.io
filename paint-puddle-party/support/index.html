Paint Puddle Party Support

Paint Puddle Party is a 3 v 3 paint game. Paint the floor in your team's colour, throw your paint glob to pull rivals onto your paint, and be the last team standing.

How do I play?

Tap the floor to run there, and double tap to dash. Drag and let go to throw your paint glob. A glob that catches a rival pulls them to you. Standing on the other team's paint while its owner is close costs health.

What are the things on the floor?

  • Heart: gives health back
  • Bolt: makes you run faster for a few seconds
  • Paint splat: paints a big patch in your colour
  • Paint bucket: refills your paint

How do I change how my kid looks?

Open the closet from the home screen. You earn new pieces by playing.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. There is no account, and every match is against computer players on your device.

Does it cost anything?

No. The game is free and sells nothing.
