Privacy Policy

Effective Effective November 10, 2026

Paint Puddle Party is a paint game for iPhone and iPad. It collects no data. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

What the game asks you

The first time you open the game it asks for the year you were born and lets you pick a name from two lists of words. You cannot type a name. Both are saved on your device and are never sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • Your birth year and the name you picked
  • Your kid's look: colour, tool and the pieces you have earned
  • Your progress, match results and settings

If you back up your device with iCloud, Apple may include this game's saved data in your own backup. The developer cannot see it.

What the game does not do

  • No account and no sign-in
  • No ads and no tracking
  • No analytics
  • No purchases
  • No chat and no messages between players
  • No camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location

Every match is played against computer players on your device. The game does not connect to a game server.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the game from your device and everything it saved is removed.

Changes

If a later version adds online play or purchases, this page will be updated before that version is released.
