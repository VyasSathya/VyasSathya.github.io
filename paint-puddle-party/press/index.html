Paint Puddle Party

Paint the floor, pull rivals onto your colour and be the last team standing.

Facts

What it is
A 3 v 3 paint arena. Paint the floor in your team's colour, throw a paint glob that grabs a rival and pulls them onto your colour, and the last team standing wins.
How you play
Single player against computer players. Works offline.
Price
Free. No ads, nothing to buy.

iPhone and iPad
