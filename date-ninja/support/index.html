Date Ninja Support

Profile cards are tossed up. Swipe right through the hotties, left through the uggos, and stay away from the red flag bombs.

How does scoring work?

Every right-way swipe is a match and scores. Hit several cards in one swipe for a combo. A wrong-way swipe costs 10 points and breaks your combo. In Classic, every card you let fall costs one of three lives, and touching a red flag bomb ends the run. In Arcade and Zen the clock runs out instead, and a red flag costs 10 points.

What are roses for?

You earn roses by playing. Spend them in the shop on swipe trails, backgrounds and power-ups. Roses cannot be bought.

Leaderboards

Sign in to Game Center to see how your best scores compare with friends and everyone else. Tap the trophy on the home screen.
