Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Date Ninja is a swipe game. There is no account, and nothing you do in the game is sent to the developer.

This policy covers the Date Ninja app for iPhone (bundle ID com.vyassathya.dateninja) and for Android (package com.vyassathya.dateninja), developed by Vyas Sathya.

What we collect

Nothing. Date Ninja has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server of its own. Your scores, settings, roses and unlocks stay on your device.

If you email us, we keep that email to answer it.

Game Center (iPhone only)

On iPhone, if you are signed in to Game Center, your best scores are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards so you can compare them with friends and other players. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the developer does not receive your Apple Account details. The Android version has no leaderboards and does not connect to the internet.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app makes a score card on your device and opens your phone's share sheet. Where it goes is your choice; nothing is sent to the developer.

Camera, microphone, photos and location

Date Ninja does not use them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your scores, settings and unlocks from your device. On iPhone, scores on Game Center leaderboards are managed by Apple in the Game Center settings.

Children

Date Ninja does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@vyasapps.dev or through the Date Ninja support page.
