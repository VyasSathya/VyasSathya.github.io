Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

K-Sing Karaoke lets you sing K-pop songs and score your singing. Scoring happens on your iPhone. We keep only what the app needs to work, we never sell data, and there is no tracking or advertising.

This policy covers the K-Sing Karaoke app for iPhone (bundle ID com.idolsing.app), developed by Vyas Sathya.

What we collect

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple, we receive a user ID and, if you choose to share it, your email address. They are stored with our database provider, Supabase, to keep your songs, progress and purchases with your account.
  • Your progress. Songs you sang, scores and streaks, linked to your account.
  • Song requests and reports. When you search for a song, request one or report a problem with one, we receive that request.
  • Party mode. When you start or join a party, the room code and each player's song and member picks pass through our server so everyone's screen stays in step.
  • If you email us, we keep that email to answer it.

Microphone, camera and your own song files

If you use your own copy of a song, the app only reads its length on your iPhone to check it is that song, then deletes its copy. The file is never uploaded.

The microphone is used to score your singing. Your voice is processed on your iPhone and is not recorded or uploaded for scoring. The camera is used only when you record a clip of yourself. Clips stay on your device until you save them to Photos or share them, for example to TikTok, which you do yourself.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • RevenueCat: keeps track of your K-Sing Plus purchase, using your App Store purchase history and your account ID. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • YouTube: songs play in YouTube's player inside the app, and song search uses YouTube's search. YouTube's terms and Google's privacy policy apply to that player.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and App Store purchases.

Purchases

K-Sing Plus and the Party Pass are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app, in Me > Settings > Delete account. That deletes your account and the data linked to it from our database. You can also email us to ask for deletion. Deleting the app removes what is stored on your device.

Children

K-Sing Karaoke is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@kpopkaraoke.app or through the K-Sing Karaoke support page.
