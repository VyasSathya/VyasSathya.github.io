Delete your account

In the app

Open Me > Settings > Delete account and confirm. This deletes your K-Sing Karaoke data and any files you uploaded, and signs you out.

If no other app of ours uses your sign-in, your login is deleted too. If another of our apps uses the same sign-in, you choose: delete only your K-Sing Karaoke data and keep the login for that app, or delete your account everywhere.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Without the app

Email support@kpopkaraoke.app from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We delete your account and its data within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in details, your progress and scores, song requests and reports you sent, and files you uploaded. Anything stored only on your phone is removed when you delete the app.
