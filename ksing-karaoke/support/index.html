K-Sing Karaoke Support

K-Sing Karaoke is karaoke for K-pop: pick a song, pick the member you sing as, and see how close you got. Singing is free.

What does K-Sing Plus add?

Saving and sharing your takes, your score history, and practice tools such as slow-down and key change. Plus is monthly or yearly, and the yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. The Party Pass unlocks party mode on the TV for 48 hours and does not renew.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Plus screen in the app and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you used to buy it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose K-Sing Karaoke and tap Cancel Subscription. Plus stays active until the end of the current period. To avoid being charged after the free trial, cancel at least a day before it ends.

On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, choose K-Sing Karaoke and tap Cancel subscription.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Me > Settings > Delete account. You can also email us.
