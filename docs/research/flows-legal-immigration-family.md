# Client-facing flows: immigration, family law, estate planning (2026-10-20)

Condensed from two research passes (vendor help centers, product pages, App Store listings and reviews).
Sources are the vendor pages named in each line; reviews are App Store / G2 / GetApp / Trustpilot.

## Immigration

### Docketwise (small and mid-size firms; family-based, naturalization, some employment)
Source: supportcenter.docketwise.com (client portal and Smart Forms articles).
- Invite email "Confirm your email and set password" > create password > login at client.docketwise.com, optional TOTP 2FA set by the firm.
- Dashboard: five cards (Tasks, Forms, USCIS Receipts, Invoices, Files), each with See All; side nav adds Messages.
- Tasks: firm-assigned list, client ticks complete.
- Forms (Smart Forms questionnaires): tabbed, 12 languages chosen by the firm at invite and switchable by the client; answers fill the English USCIS forms; status Sent > Accepted > Returned for Review; per-question comments if allowed; biographic data entered once across combined petitions.
- USCIS Receipts: receipt numbers with a status that LINKS OUT to the USCIS site (no in-portal status, processing time, RFE or visa bulletin).
- Invoices: list, download, Pay (card or eCheck).
- Files: folders, upload, move, bulk download.
- Messages: secure threads; the email only says a message arrived.
- Reviews (firm staff): clients find forms easy; reminders weak; want a translate button; autosave without undo.

### INSZoom (large firms, corporate immigration)
Source: success.mitratech.com help articles.
- Foreign National Portal sections: To Do (questionnaires, forms to sign, documents; finished items disappear), Documents (passport, visa, I-94, EAD with validity and expired status), Cases (status and process steps), case emails/notes, Calendar, Travel, Dependents, corporate news/policies.
- Firm toggles sections; e-consent terms gate; machine-translated questionnaires.
- Reviews: dated, slow; practitioners say employees forget the portal and email documents instead.

### Envoy Global (employer-sponsored cases)
Source: help.envoyglobal.com, App Store id1523370602.
- Case stages: Initial Information Gathering > Attorney Review > In Process > Sent to Government > Complete.
- Questionnaire asks for uploads inline; attorneys add action items; Communication Center; personal document library with I-94 from CBP; expiration tracking.
- App 2.5 stars: valued for visa expiration dates; login/update bugs.

### LollyLaw
- Intake in 20+ languages, uploads, payment status, live chat while filling forms, SMS updates and reminders, pay by text/QR.

### Patterns
- Home is a to-do list plus case status; documents are requested per item; questionnaires in the client's language.
- Gap: nobody shows USCIS status, processing time, RFE or interview date inside the portal.
- Clients forget uploads; reminders and SMS matter more than the portal home.

## Family law

### OurFamilyWizard (court-ordered co-parenting; attorneys get a professional view)
Source: ourfamilywizard.com product pages and help center; App Store 4.6 (about 56K ratings).
- Messages: permanent, no edit/delete; sent and first-viewed times; ToneMeter flags tone before sending.
- Calendar: parenting-time schedule colored by parent, templates (2-2-3, week on/off), swap requests with approval and a log.
- Expense Log: amount, category, receipt photo, percentage split, request/refuse/pay, reports.
- Journal: private by default, share to chosen people; GPS check-ins at exchanges.
- Info Bank: health, school, activities, sizes.
- Attorney: requests access, the parent approves, read-only "view as client", downloadable reports with read receipts.
- Complaints: no badge notifications, limited expense notes, both parents must pay.

### TalkingParents
Source: talkingparents.com features and help center; App Store 4.4 (about 29K ratings).
- Secure messaging, recorded calls, shared calendar, payments, info library, private journal, vault.
- Records: signed PDF with authentication code or certified printed records; lawyers receive records by emailed link, no login.
- Complaints: crashes, both parents pay, records cost after the case.

### Patterns
- The record is the product: nothing editable after it is seen, timestamps on everything, exportable signed records.
- Attorney access by client approval, read-only.

## Estate planning

### WealthCounsel Client Intake
- Emailed link to a mobile-friendly questionnaire: people (spouse, children, fiduciaries), assets, documents; autosaves; firm sees progress; data flows into Wealth Docx drafting.
- Complaint (from competitor): cannot edit after submission.

### DecisionVault
- Client portal at own pace: people with relationships, financial information, itemized asset inventory; can return after the design meeting; firm can brand and edit questions, request files; asset export, funding instructions, plan summaries and diagrams.

### Vanilla
- Shared visuals: plan flowchart, who-gets-what waterfall, family tree, beneficiary and fiduciary summaries, one-page plan snapshot.

### Patterns
- One guided questionnaire (people, fiduciaries, assets, uploads) that saves progress and stays editable.
- After signing: a funding checklist (retitle accounts into the trust) and plain visual summaries.
