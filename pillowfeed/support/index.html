Pillowfeed Support

Pillowfeed is a private feed for the photos and videos you save, kept behind Face ID. Add media from your Photos library or a link, scroll through it like a feed, and keep it in sync across your devices with your own iCloud.

Where is my media stored?

On your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud if you turn on iCloud sync. Nothing is uploaded to the developer, and there is no account to create. See the privacy policy for details.

How does Free up space work?

Free up space is part of Pillowfeed Pro. It moves your collection to your own private iCloud and removes it from your iPhone, while everything stays in your feed and plays from iCloud when you swipe to it. A phone copy is removed only after its iCloud copy is saved, and on recent versions of iOS the work keeps going after you leave the app, with progress shown in a Live Activity.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Pillowfeed Pro screen in the app and tap Restore at the bottom. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Pillowfeed and tap Cancel Subscription. Pro stays active until the end of the current billing period. The lifetime purchase has no renewal to cancel.

How do I move to a new phone?

On your old phone, use iCloud in Pillowfeed (Storage & backup) and let the backup finish. On the new phone, sign in with the same Apple Account, install Pillowfeed, open Storage & backup and tap Restore an existing iCloud collection. Then tap Restore on the Pillowfeed Pro screen to bring back your purchase.
