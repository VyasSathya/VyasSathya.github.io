Round Em Up Support

The animals have broken out and are wandering toward the fence. Drag to move the cowboy; his tools fire on their own and turn the animals back toward the pen.

How do I lose?

Every animal that crosses the fence costs health. Bigger animals cost more. When the health bar is empty the run ends.

How do I level up?

An animal drops a gem where you turn it for home. Gems dropped farther from the pen are worth more. Walk over them to collect them, then pick a new tool or a power-up.

What are the barrels?

Walk into a barrel to break it open. A hay bale restores health, a horseshoe pulls every gem to you, sundown stops every animal for a moment, and a cow bell turns everything near you toward the pen.

What are the chests?

A chest appears when a tool is at level 5 and you hold its matching power-up. Walk onto it to turn that tool into its stronger form.
