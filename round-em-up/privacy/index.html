Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Round Em Up is a herding game. There is no account, and nothing you do in the game is sent to the developer.

This policy covers the Round Em Up app for iPhone (bundle ID com.vyassathya.roundemup) and for Android (package com.vyassathya.roundemup), developed by Vyas Sathya.

What we collect

Nothing. Round Em Up has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server of its own. The game does not connect to the internet.

If you email us, we keep that email to answer it.

Camera, microphone, photos, contacts and location

Round Em Up does not use them.

Deleting your data

The game keeps nothing about you. Deleting the app removes it from your device.

Children

Round Em Up does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@vyasapps.dev or through the Round Em Up support page.
