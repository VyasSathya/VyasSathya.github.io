Band Battler

Two bands play the same song at the same time. Every note a musician plays is their move. This is the baseline build: placeholder looks, real rules.

  • 1You get 10 gold every gig. Musicians cost 3, a reroll costs 1. Gold you don't spend is lost.
  • 2Tap a shop card to hear its part, then tap a square (or Buy). Tap your own musicians to move or sell them. The front row, next to the chords, gets hit first.
  • 3Short notes hit. Held low notes shield. Swelling chords heal. Stabs splash. A choir makes your band hit harder. A bent note slows. A huge drum makes its target rest.
  • 4The yellow bar fills as a musician plays. When it glows, tap them to start their solo on the next bar. Tapping on a beat adds 25%. Not tapping is fine: the solo starts a bar later on its own.
  • 5Three copies make a ★2 with a fuller part. Two or three different musicians of a family turn on its bonus.
  • 6Ten gigs, four hearts. Your song grows from intro to final chorus as your band grows. Rivals are bands saved at the same gig.

The ♪ button switches sound sets and loads your own stems for an A/B test.
