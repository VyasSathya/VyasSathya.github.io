Questions

Do I need a new website?

No. The Portal plan adds a Client login to the site you have, on Wix, Squarespace or anything else, matched to your colors and fonts.

What do my clients need?

Just the link you send them. It opens in any browser and can be added to their phone's home screen for notifications.

What does it connect to?

Your existing tools stay where they are. The portal works beside your booking calendar, invoices and email instead of replacing them, and is connected to them where they allow it.

Who owns it?

Your domain, your content and your client data are yours. If you stop, you get a full export of your projects, and Website + Portal clients get the site's code.

Is my clients' information private?

Each client sees only their own project, through a private link you can turn off at any time. Nothing is shared with other businesses or used to train AI.

How long does setup take?

The free demo takes two days. Setup takes about a week once you send your branding and current projects.

Can I cancel?

Yes, month to month after setup.
