K-Dance Academy Support

K-Dance Academy teaches K-pop dances step by step, with a coach who breaks each move down and an optional camera check of your form. One full lesson a day and the chorus of every song are free.

What does K-Dance Pro add?

Slow-down, section loops, mirror view and full songs, on every lesson. Pro is a yearly plan with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime unlock.

Does the camera record me?

No. The camera reads your position on your iPhone to compare your moves with the dance. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. See the privacy policy.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the K-Dance Pro screen in the app and tap Restore at the bottom. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose K-Dance Academy and tap Cancel Subscription. Pro stays active until the end of the current billing period. To avoid being charged after the free trial, cancel at least a day before it ends. The lifetime unlock has no renewal to cancel.
