Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

K-Dance Academy teaches K-pop dances step by step. The camera and microphone work on your iPhone only. There is no account, and nothing you do in a lesson is sent to the developer.

This policy covers the K-Dance Academy app for iPhone (bundle ID com.sparktory.kpopdance), developed by Vyas Sathya.

What we collect

No personal data. K-Dance Academy has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your video, your voice and your lesson progress stay on your device.

If you email us, we keep that email to answer it.

Camera

When you turn the camera on in a lesson, the app reads your body position on your iPhone to show how your moves match the dance. Camera frames are processed on the device and are never recorded, stored or uploaded.

Microphone

When you use voice commands (such as "slower" or "again"), speech is recognized on your iPhone to control the lesson. Audio is not recorded, stored or sent to the developer.

Network use

  • Lessons and music. The app downloads dance lessons and song sections from our server. The request names the song; it carries no account or personal information. Like any web server, ours sees your IP address while serving the download and does not keep it with any profile.
  • Purchases. Handled by Apple (see below).

Purchases

K-Dance Pro and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple through the App Store. To know which features you have unlocked, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your App Store purchase history and a random app user ID, not your name, email or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your lesson progress and settings from your device. There is no account or server copy to delete.

Children

K-Dance Academy does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@kpopdanceacademy.com or through the K-Dance Academy support page.
